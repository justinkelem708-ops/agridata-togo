AgriData Togo

AI-Powered Agricultural Price Intelligence for West Africa

Independent Research Project — Togo, 2026

Mission

AgriData Togo is an independent data science research initiative focused on modeling agricultural price volatility and post-harvest loss across the five regions of Togo. Using machine learning and a national network of field data collectors, we build predictive systems that give smallholder farmers and institutional actors a 30-60 day price forecast horizon.

This project addresses a critical information asymmetry: farmers sell at harvest when prices are lowest, while intermediaries hold stock and sell at peak prices. Data-driven early warning systems can rebalance this dynamic.

Research Scope

Target commodities : Maize, Soybean, Sesame, Sorghum, Cowpea. Data collected : weekly market prices, volumes, storage conditions, seller profiles, buyer-seller price spreads (information asymmetry index).

External data integrated : FAO GIEWS historical prices, World Bank agricultural indicators, OpenWeatherMap climate variables.

Primary research question : Can a multi-source machine learning system predict agricultural price instability 30-60 days ahead in low-infrastructure West African markets ?
